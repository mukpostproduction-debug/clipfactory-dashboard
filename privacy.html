ClipFactory Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

ClipFactory is an internal studio automation application developed for Muk Post Production. It connects to the YouTube Data API solely to schedule, manage, and audit autonomous video uploads for authorized studio and partner channels.

Data Handling & Privacy

ClipFactory does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties. All authentication credentials and API tokens remain stored locally on the user's dedicated hardware.

Contact

For questions or support, contact Muk Post Production at mukpost.com.
